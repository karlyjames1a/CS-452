## Project Summary

The travel planning app helps users discover things to do in a city they are traveling to and organize locations into an itinerary. Users can search for a destination, explore local recommendations gathered through APIs, and save places they’re interested in. An AI will use the saved locations to suggest an itinerary. 

## Goals

- Make planning vacations less stressful by providing activities and locations that reflect the user's interests.
- Generate an itinerary that can be manually edited by the user.
